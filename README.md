# ⛳ Daily Golf

A new 9-hole course every day, the same for everyone. Built as one HTML file with no downloads or libraries.

**Play:** https://jamesgregory5-svg.github.io/daily-golf/ (or download `index.html` and open it in any browser)

## How to play
- **Aim:** tap or drag on the course (← → keys on a keyboard). The dashed line shows how far your club goes at full power.
- **Swing:** press **Swing** (or Space), then press again to stop the power meter.
- **Wind:** watch the arrow. It pushes the ball, and high shots drift more.
- **Clubs:** use ‹ › (↑ ↓ keys). Rough and sand cut your distance.
- **Hazards:** water and out of bounds cost +1 stroke. Trees stop the ball.
- **Putting is automatic:** stop inside the inner ring for 1 putt, the middle ring for 2, and anywhere else on the green for 3. Roll it in the cup to hole out.

Your daily round, streak, and best score are saved in your browser. When you finish, copy your emoji scorecard to share it. Practice and random courses don't count toward your stats.
